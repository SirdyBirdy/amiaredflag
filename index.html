What is a red flag, really?

A red flag in relationships is a warning sign — a behaviour or pattern that suggests emotional unavailability, manipulation, avoidance, or general chaos energy. The tricky part? Most red flags don't know they're red flags.

The checklist above covers the most common ones: avoidant attachment, passive-aggressive communication, breadcrumbing, commitment-phobia, and the classic "i just don't do labels" defence. If you checked more than seven, we gently suggest calling someone who charges by the hour.
